Keep the rising notes keeps the first drawn note, then only notes whose color is higher than the preceding drawn note. The comparison uses the original phrase, not the cut result. Undo restores the phrase.

Keep the falling notes keeps the first drawn note, then only notes whose color is lower than the preceding drawn note. The comparison uses the original phrase. Undo restores the phrase.

Keep color changes keeps the first drawn note and each note that differs in color from the preceding drawn note. Seeds of the same color sing the same pitch. Undo restores the repeats.

Keep the high points keeps both phrase ends and any middle note whose color is higher than both its neighbors in the original drawn phrase. Equal colors are not peaks. Undo restores the other notes.

Keep the low points keeps both phrase ends and any middle note whose color is lower than both its neighbors in the original drawn phrase. Equal colors are not valleys. Undo restores the other notes.

Keep each color’s entrance keeps the first drawn seed of each of the five colors, in its original order. It makes a short pitch outline, not a new preset. Undo restores the full phrase.
